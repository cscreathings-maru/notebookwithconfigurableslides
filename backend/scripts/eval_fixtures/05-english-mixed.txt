Digital Transformation Roadmap 2027

The bank will consolidate its three legacy customer portals into a single
platform. Phase one covers authentication and account overview, targeted for
Q1. Phase two covers payments and transfers, targeted for Q2.

Estimated cost saving is Rp 120 billion annually from decommissioning the
legacy systems and reducing duplicated support effort.

Key risks: data migration completeness, regulator approval timing, and change
fatigue among branch staff who have already absorbed two major system changes
this year.

Mitigation: a parallel-run period of eight weeks, early engagement with the
regulator, and a dedicated branch enablement team.
